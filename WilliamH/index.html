Donation Recommendations

Beyond William's passions in physical theater and fitness, William was also attentive to the needs of youth. During his time developing skills as a circus arts performer, he worked for Circ Esteem a 501(c)(3) helping Chicago's youth build confidence, agency, and community through circus arts. Click here to donate and support their work.

Additionally, please consider participating in actions by or donating to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention. If you donate via Will's campaign, a portion of the proceeds will support the AFSP Michigan Chapter.
